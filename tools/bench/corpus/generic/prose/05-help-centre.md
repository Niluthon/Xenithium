# Help Centre

Step-by-step answers to the most common questions. If you cannot find what you need, contact our support team.

## How to reset your password

This article explains how to reset your password. It takes about 4 minutes. You need to be signed in to do this.

### On the website

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Click your profile picture in the top right corner.
3. Select **Settings**, then open the **Account** tab.
4. Find the section called **Reset your password** and click it.
5. Follow the instructions on the screen, then click **Save**.

### In the mobile app

1. Open the app and tap the menu icon in the bottom right corner.
2. Tap **Settings**, then **Account**.
3. Tap **Reset your password** and follow the steps.
4. Tap **Done** to save your changes.

### Good to know

- Changes are saved at once and apply to every device you use.
- If you are part of a team, only an administrator may be able to reset your password.
- You will receive a confirmation email after the change. If you did not make the change, contact support immediately.

### Still need help?

If these steps did not work, our support team is happy to help. Write to us from the **Contact** page, and include a short description of the problem and, if possible, a screenshot. We usually answer within 24 hours.
