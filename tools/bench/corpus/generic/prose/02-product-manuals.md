# Product Manuals and Frequently Asked Questions

Each section below is the short manual of one product: what is in the box, how to set it up, how to use it every day, how to keep it clean, and the answers to the questions customers ask most often.

## Wireless Headphones WI-120

Thank you for choosing the Wireless Headphones WI-120. Please read this manual carefully before using the product for the first time, and keep it in a safe place for future reference.

### What is in the box

- One wireless headphones
- One USB-C cable
- This user manual
- A warranty card

### Setting it up

1. Unpack the wireless headphones and remove all protective film and packaging.
2. Place it on a flat, dry and stable surface, away from heat sources and direct sunlight.
3. Connect the wireless headphones to the power supply and wait until the indicator light turns green.
4. Download the companion app, create an account and follow the instructions on the screen to pair the device.
5. When the app confirms the connection, the device is ready to use.

### Everyday use

Press the power button once to turn the wireless headphones on, and press it again to turn it off. Hold the button for 10 seconds to restart the device. You can change the settings in the app at any time; the device remembers them even when it is unplugged. For the best results, update the firmware whenever the app tells you that a new version is available.

### Cleaning and care

Always unplug the wireless headphones before cleaning it. Wipe the outside with a soft, slightly damp cloth and dry it afterwards. Do not use strong cleaning products, and never put the device in water or in the dishwasher. Clean it about once every week, or more often if you use it every day.

### Frequently asked questions

**The wireless headphones does not turn on. What should I do?**
Check that the power cable is connected properly and that the socket works. If the device still does not turn on, hold the power button for ten seconds to reset it.

**The app cannot find the device.**
Make sure that Bluetooth and location are enabled on your phone, and that the device is within a few metres. Restart both the phone and the device, then try again.

**How long is the warranty?**
The wireless headphones comes with a 2-year warranty from the date of purchase. Keep your receipt, as you will need it to make a claim.

**Can I use it outdoors?**
No. The wireless headphones is designed for indoor use only. Moisture and extreme temperatures can damage it.

### Technical data

- Power: 65 W
- Weight: 0.5 kg
- Dimensions: 16 x 14 x 22 cm
- Colour: white
