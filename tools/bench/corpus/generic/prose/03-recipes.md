# Simple Recipes for Every Day

These recipes use ingredients you can find in any supermarket. Each one lists the ingredients first, then the steps in order.

## Tomato Soup

Serves 4. Preparation time: 15 minutes. Cooking time: 15 minutes.

This tomato soup is easy to make and always popular with family and friends. It keeps well in the fridge for two or three days, and it tastes even better the next day.

### Ingredients

- 2 cream
- 200 g carrots
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 2 olive oil
- 250 ml parsley
- 2 basil
- 250 ml lemon
- 1 rice

### Method

1. Preheat the oven to 160 degrees Celsius, if the recipe needs it.
2. Wash and chop the vegetables, and weigh out all the other ingredients.
3. Heat a little oil or butter in a large pan over a medium heat.
4. Add the onion and garlic and cook gently for five minutes, stirring from time to time, until soft.
5. Add the remaining ingredients one by one and stir well.
6. Cook for about 15 minutes, until everything is cooked through.
7. Taste, and season with salt and pepper.
8. Serve warm, with bread or a fresh green salad.

### Tips

- You can make the tomato soup a day ahead and warm it up before serving.
- For a vegetarian version, use vegetable stock instead of chicken stock.
- Leftovers can be frozen for up to three months.
