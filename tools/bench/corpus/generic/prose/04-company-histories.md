# Company Histories

Short histories of local companies, from their founding to the present day.

## Northwind Traders

Northwind Traders was founded in 1911 by Quinn Miller in Athens. It began as a small family business with only 2 employees, working from a single room above a shop. In the first years the company struggled, but Quinn believed in the idea and worked long hours to keep it going.

### Growth

In 1924, the company moved into a larger building and hired its first managers. Demand grew steadily, and by 1941 Northwind Traders had opened offices in 3 cities. The company became known for the quality of its work and for treating its customers and employees fairly.

### Difficult years

Like many companies, Northwind Traders faced hard times. During an economic crisis, sales fell sharply and the company had to close several branches. The management team cut costs, focused on its most important customers and invested in new technology. Within a few years, the company had recovered and was growing again.

### Today

Today Northwind Traders employs more than 3,200 people and serves customers in 32 countries. The company is still owned by the Miller family, and its headquarters remain in Athens. It supports local schools and charities, and every year it offers apprenticeships to young people from the region.
