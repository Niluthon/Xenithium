# A Traveller's Guide to the Towns of the Region

This guide describes the towns of the region one by one: how to get there, what to see, where to eat and when to go.

## Ashford

Ashford is a small town in the mountain foothills with a population of about 14,000 people. It is known for its friendly atmosphere, its well-kept streets and its long history. Many visitors come for a day, but the town is best enjoyed over a weekend, when there is time to walk slowly and see everything without hurrying.

### Getting there

The easiest way to reach Ashford is by train. Trains leave from the main station every 90 minutes and the journey takes about 2 hours. If you travel by car, follow the main road and take the exit marked Ashford. There is a large car park near the centre, and parking costs about 3 euros per day. Buses also run from the nearby cities several times a day.

### What to see

Start your visit at the town hall, which is the heart of the town. From there it is a short walk to the old mill, one of the most popular places in Ashford. In the afternoon, visit the riverside promenade, where you can enjoy a beautiful view of the surrounding countryside. Guided tours start every day at ten o'clock in the morning and last about two hours.

### Where to eat

The town has many small restaurants and cafés. Local specialities include berry tarts and local cheese, which you can find in most restaurants. For a traditional meal, try one of the family-run inns near the main square. Prices are reasonable: a main course costs between 10 and 19 euros.

### When to go

The best time to visit Ashford is in autumn, when the weather is pleasant and the town holds its yearly festival. The festival has music, food stalls and a parade through the old streets. Hotels fill up quickly during the festival, so it is a good idea to book a room a few weeks in advance.

### Tips

- Wear comfortable shoes: the streets of the old town are paved with stones.
- Most shops close at 6 o'clock in the evening and on Sundays.
- The tourist office next to the town hall has free maps and can help you find a place to stay.
- Bring a camera: the view from the riverside promenade at sunset is one of the best in the region.
